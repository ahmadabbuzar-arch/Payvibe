About PayVibe

PayVibe is a UPI payment companion that hands off payments to your installed UPI apps using the standard UPI deep-link. PayVibe is not a bank, not a UPI network member, and never processes money itself.

PayVibe never asks for your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, or banking passwords. Every payment is confirmed inside your own UPI app.

The Payment Vibe score is entertainment only — it is not a credit score, risk score, or any kind of financial rating.
